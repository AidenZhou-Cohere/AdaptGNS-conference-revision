# Prospective Goop3D graph-support study

This is a separate Goop3D study. It does not revise or reinterpret the frozen
WaterDrop, Sand or Goop2D training protocols, checkpoints, failed gates or results.
The endpoint is **unset** in this draft. Only a later root-issued endpoint plan,
training admission and scientific release may choose and bind it. The saved
training source and this protocol then remain byte-frozen.

## Verification and time accounting

The process loop derives narrowly from the reviewed scientific supervisor and
uses the unchanged identity-aware lifecycle's wait4/reaping/cleanup functions.
Record PID,parent,start ticks,argv,assigned GPU,stdout/stderr,external child lifetime,
Linux peak RSS,signal history,launch skew and observations. Stop only owned
unreaped children whose captured identity matches; retain any cleanup refusal or
unreaped-child record. Full CPU checkpoint audits run after every wave child is
reaped, avoiding audit contention with that wave's training.

Every graph-update row must exist and match the deterministic host sample and
per-example coin schedule. Paired arms must share all samples,noises,LRs,initial
model/empty Adam/CPU-CUDA RNG, native graph hashes and counts, noisy states,
coin/pair RNG material and annulus budgets. Optional counts may differ according
to the declared arm. Reconstruct exact arbitrary-endpoint log/checkpoint cadence,
including a final nonmultiple endpoint. Hash every retained checkpoint. Deserialize
initial/final checkpoints on CPU and check model configuration, tensor restoration,
finite model/Adam values, every Adam step and serialized RNG. Intermediate payloads
are retained and byte-hashed, not separately deserialized by this supervisor.

The initial tensor pairing audit occurs after training; no before-step1 barrier
is claimed. Record whether the initial pointer was observed live. A fast run may
finish before the polling loop observes that pointer; its immutable step0 artifact,
sole fresh attempt and complete source/configuration evidence are still audited.

Preserve both wave wall times and child lifetimes. Child lifetimes overlap within
a wave and include observer lag; their sum is not elapsed wall time. Guarded-time
sums from logged rows describe only those logged updates. Never infer all-update
timing from a sparse log or report capacity timing as scientific runtime.

After all six verified endpoints, the supervisor writes complete cohort/audit
**candidates** with schemas compatible with the reviewed evaluator. They remain
`issued_by=scientific_supervisor_not_root`, with evaluation admission false. Root
must independently review and freeze the complete cohort and actual endpoint
before any final evaluation or reserved-test source access.
