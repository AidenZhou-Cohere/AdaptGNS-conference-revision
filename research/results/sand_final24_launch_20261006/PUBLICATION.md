# Sand evaluation launch evidence

This package preserves the reviewed source and completed startup of the original six-model Sand evaluation, sessions A51595 and B12559. The evaluation is still running at publication preparation. No final accuracy, evaluation completion or stopped-analysis admission is asserted.

The original candidate README, manifest and all 158 payloads remain byte-identical; statements that publication had not occurred describe that candidate at creation. The added independent curation report verifies 690 source and preservation checks. Its checker is also included. The already completed release/startup reviews are preserved without rerunning them.

The full comparison retains six 100k models, six policies, 24 stages, H314 and 1,080 planned autonomous outcomes. Every timeout, guard and unexecuted cell must remain in eventual analysis. Neither elapsed stage quotas nor startup success guarantees full-horizon completion.

Raw transport logs and command captures, credential configuration, raw tool responses, model/array contents and mutable evaluation products are uniformly excluded and catalogued. Source and declared invocation definitions are included. This evidence package does not authorize replaying its historical controls.
