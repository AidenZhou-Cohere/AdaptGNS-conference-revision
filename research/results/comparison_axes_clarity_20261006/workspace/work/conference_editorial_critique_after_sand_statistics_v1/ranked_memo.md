## 2. Name the constructive intervention before touring results

**Risk:** The introduction opens as a new residual-selector paper, while its strongest positive evidence concerns training support. A reviewer may miss the constructive contribution and see an unsuccessful selector with extensive diagnostics.

**Change:** Replace “We investigate this connection...” with:

> We pair base-only training with exposure to random, budgeted graph expansion, then freeze each simulator and compare allocation policies. This separates learning to use additional messages from choosing where to add them. Goop and Sand train both arms from initialization; WaterDrop uses paired continuations. Matched observed histories test placement, and full rollouts test feedback. Earlier objective and action-benefit controls explain why residual prediction alone need not identify useful interactions.

Keep the following result paragraph, including adverse Sand evidence. This does not claim that graph augmentation, faithful regression or the elementary top-budget bound is individually new.

**Risk:** Table 1 uses “base” for both training and evaluation; Table 2 gives training effects, not absolute policy rankings.
