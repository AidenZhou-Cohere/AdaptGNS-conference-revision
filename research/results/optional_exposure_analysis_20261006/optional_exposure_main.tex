\subsection{Where does the sparse-policy error gap appear?}
We partition the recorded risk-minus-random error gap by whether particles
receive optional messages under neither, only the left, only the right, or
both actions. The whole gap is $0.486\pm0.111$ / $0.860\pm0.102$
in normalized coordinate MSE $\times10^3$ (faithful/NLL; three seed means
and sample SDs). Positive values favor random.
Particles covered by both actions give the largest positive contribution
in every seed of both objectives.
Relative to random, risk has greater prediction-change cost and greater
alignment with the base residual, but the cost increase exceeds the
alignment increase in every seed. These are observed algebraic components.
The four unconditional contributions sum to this gap; Appendix~\ref{sec:optional-exposure} reports
all groups and the two speed controls. Exposure is defined by the policy,
so the partition is descriptive. With ten message-passing blocks, particles
in the neither group can still be affected through their neighbors.
