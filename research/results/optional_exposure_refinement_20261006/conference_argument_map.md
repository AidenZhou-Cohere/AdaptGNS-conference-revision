Prepared after the three exploratory full-model follow-ups and before the faithful 110k continuation outcomes. This is an interpretation and writing plan, not a change to any frozen experiment. The registered title/abstract remain unchanged pending author comparison.

The strongest supported contribution is a controlled investigation of when residual prediction fails to supply an allocation signal. Exact budgets, matched histories, objective controls, graph-convention controls, actual-action labels and complete autonomous failure accounting make that question testable. ## Interpret the paired continuation before choosing the narrative

Always report all five policy outcomes for both training arms, all three seed effects, and the frozen paired interaction. Do not turn the following possibilities into additional selection criteria or change the planned population.

- If graph exposure improves all policies similarly, the result supports a training-support effect on prediction. It does not specifically validate risk allocation.
- If the risk-minus-random interaction improves while risk still loses to random, the deficit narrows; superiority remains unsupported. Report the absolute errors alongside the interaction.
- If risk becomes more accurate on observed histories, any allocation claim is conditional on these continued faithful models and the inspected split. Autonomous failures, full-horizon error and boundaries determine how far that claim extends.
- If an autonomous arm fails, retain its failure indicator and accepted-prefix diagnostics, leaving affected all-sample full-horizon means undefined. A favorable survivor mean is not the predeclared outcome.
- If expansion remains harmful after the fixed exposure continuation, conclude that this intervention did not resolve the deficit. Ten thousand continuation updates and one augmentation distribution cannot rule out other training regimes or establish a universal impossibility.
- If errors improve while physical excursions or measured costs worsen, report the tradeoff. Do not use lower edge totals on different predicted geometries as an equal-state speedup.

The highest-value remaining numerical work is therefore the current native autonomous cohort followed, if feasible, by the reviewed matched continuation and its complete endpoint evaluation. A test-optimized damping coefficient would restate an inspected quadratic identity; a new learned gate adds fitting and rollout validation costs. Neither currently displaces the paired training control.
