## Estimands and null rules

Validation and test are separate populations. An observed unit is `(split, source_index, trajectory_id, target_frame)`; an autonomous unit is `(source_index, trajectory_id)` with all policies retained. Per-policy scalar metrics average particles/coordinates as defined above, then observed frames equally within each trajectory, then trajectories equally within original seed. Rollout trajectory metrics already average forecast steps where appropriate. Report exactly three explicit seed means, their mean and sample SD (`ddof=1`), with units and direction for each metric. Do not pool particles, frames or policy outcomes as independent replications. Unequal validation frame counts do not justify weighting trajectories by their frame counts.

For each fixed metric and population retain:

1. Absolute values for every arm, seed and policy.
2. Within each arm: risk minus random, risk minus speed, dense minus base policy.
3. Five paired arm effects: mix minus base continuation under the same inference policy.
4. Primary allocation interaction: `(risk - random)_mix - (risk - random)_base`.

Compute differences, including the four-term interaction, at the identical unit before hierarchical averaging. For error/failure metrics, negative arm effects favor mix; negative interaction means graph augmentation improves risk relative to random. Gains and correlations have different directions and must be labeled explicitly. Use `previous-observed-base-risk25` only for observed histories and `laggedrisk25` only for autonomous rollouts. These are different controllers.

Each metric/contrast carries required and defined unit counts, all three seed values, explicit null reasons and the contributing identifiers. Any missing required value makes the corresponding full-population trajectory/seed/three-seed summary null; do not average survivors or substitute zero. Failure indicators remain defined for committed outcomes, so failure rates/paired differences may be available when full-horizon errors are null. Keep every failed event, first failed phase/forecast, accepted length and raw-record link; never retry a scientific failure to improve the summary. Integrity-invalid data invalidate the affected family rather than being treated as scientific nulls.

## Interpretation and publication

This is an exploratory paired continuation from three trained faithful parents, motivated by already inspected tests. It estimates the effect of this fixed random graph-exposure continuation relative to equal-update base-only continuation, conditional on these parents and schedules. It does not establish fresh-training generality, NLL effects, independent confirmation, population significance or optimal allocation. Show all three seed effects and every predeclared policy/contrast, including adverse or null results; no p-values, best-seed selection or test-derived policy tuning.

Observed histories hold current state and candidate geometry fixed; autonomous policies and arms subsequently visit different predicted states. Their paired IDs and matched random initialization do not imply identical future edges or isolate graph placement. An observed-state improvement cannot establish rollout stability; reduced autonomous edges cannot establish more efficient placement or lower latency. Do not pool these outcomes with original no-loop100k results, native100k controls or compact pilots.

Publish into a new versioned result family: exact source/protocol/test hashes, cohort/lineage admission, coverage, all absolute/paired seed and unit values, failure/null ledgers, boundaries, allocation checks, measured timing and full raw-file inventory. A fresh output directory is mandatory; preserve every failed analysis attempt. Compact publication may omit large arrays/checkpoints only with explicit hashes/omission scope, exact scalar records and no claim of standalone raw reproducibility. Before real analysis, synthetic tests must reject swapped arms/parents/checkpoints, wrong update/schema, unequal paired schedule, tampered hashes/scalars, duplicate/unknown/missing units, uncommitted status and random-mask mismatch; cover unequal trajectory frame counts, isolated scoring failure, constant correlations, full-horizon failure nulls, defined earlier horizons, paired interaction arithmetic and failure-inclusive timing. An independent bounded saved-array audit should precede manuscript integration.
