# Paper scale, contribution and readability — October 6, 2026

Read-only review after compact-study relocation. No manuscript, table, title, abstract, experiment, queue or frozen-source changes. This assesses source text; it is not PDF inspection or a conference-readiness certification.

The paper now has a substantive experimental base: six original-width128/ten-block models, all1,000 training trajectories, three paired training seeds,27 test trajectories with995 forecasts, and matched observed-history controls. That is sufficient scale for a credible *bounded diagnostic* paper. It does not by itself make a strong AISTATS acceptance case.810 policy/trajectory outcomes are repeated observations of six trained models, not810 independent replications. The principal limits are one newly retrained material, three independent seeds per objective,100k updates versus longer historical training, and no established general accuracy–runtime advantage. More rows alone will not resolve novelty or external validity. The paired training-support control could sharpen the explanation, but its unobserved outcome must not be assumed favorable.

The current introduction and argument map give a plausible contribution: controlled evidence about when residual prediction fails to supply allocation information. Exact budgets, actual-action labels, objective/self-message controls, complete failures and measured costs support that argument. The full-model-first results and relocated compact pilot make it easier to see. I would describe an AISTATS chance as plausible but uncertain; I would not describe the present evidence as a clear acceptance case or a demonstrated superior simulator.

## Three editorial recommendations

1. **Make the inferential chain the main novelty statement.** Around line540 and the experiment opening, say what each completed control establishes: using dense benefit is not the sole explanation for weak risk–action association; removing trained self-messages is not the sole explanation for the observed risk–random ordering; absent expanded-graph training remains unresolved. This gives reviewers a concrete lesson beyond the established distinction between uncertainty and action value. Retain the observed-history scope and the lack of a causal concentration identification. Replace part of the existing control list rather than adding another paragraph of caveats.

2. **Complete the method's transition to the full-model emphasis.** The main method still spends space on the compact-only beta-NLL derivative and pilot floor/loss-scale exceptions (roughly lines551–572). Move those details to the compact appendix, with a short accurate pointer. Use the space to define the two score sources together: previous observed-base risk versus autonomous cached own-selected-graph risk. Also identify the loop-free reference, globally ordered self-message screen and native-base-prefix convention before their results. These distinctions are already correct but dispersed; a skimming reader can otherwise mistake one diagnostic for the deployed controller. This can be done with prose and appendix relocation while preserving the numerical tables.

3. **Restore concise visibility of both original historical datasets in the main text.** The current evidence-family paragraph summarizes historical WaterDrop but omits Sand, even though the full historical comparison remains in Appendix Table~tab:legacy. Add two or three sentences covering both: WaterDrop's small step200 advantage is unresolved and reverses over the full saved horizon despite fewer edges; Sand's saved adaptive means are lower than the sparse-MSE reference while edge counts rise, with error intervals crossing zero. Label the comparisons as conditional saved-array reanalysis from one model per method with unverified row pairing/training provenance. This gives the original experiments a visible role in motivating the controlled study without presenting historical Sand as a new corrected multi-seed benchmark. The full eight-row historical table can stay in the appendix.

The recent relocation concerned the **new compact pilot and its exploratory follow-ups**, not deletion of the original historical experiments. All remain in the manuscript. Keeping compact context short is justified by its few trajectories and different architecture/training setup; retaining a concise original Sand/WaterDrop comparison helps continuity and makes the tradeoffs visible. No title/registered-abstract or numerical-table edits are needed for these recommendations.
