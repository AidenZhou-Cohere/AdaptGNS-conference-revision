\section{Goop: graph exposure and placement}
\label{sec:goop-graph-exposure}
The six fresh faithful Goop models use paired seeds 0--2 and 100,000 updates. Mixed training independently expands each example with probability $1/2$ using a uniformly drawn, floor-rounded quarter of the optional annulus; the other arm uses only the native base graph. All 30 official test trajectories enter the 395-forecast evaluation, and all 150 observed test histories enter each observed-state comparison. Tables retain every policy and seed. Sample standard deviations describe the three seed means and are not confidence intervals.

All three failures occur after forecast 200. That secondary endpoint remains defined, while mixed base, dense and cached-risk full-horizon means and the cached-risk training interaction are undefined. Base and dense also fail, so these outcomes do not isolate a risk-specific instability. Clean-validation MSE and NLL improve only at seed 0, precluding a uniform calibration claim. Boundary excursions measure departure from the metadata box and do not test conservation.
