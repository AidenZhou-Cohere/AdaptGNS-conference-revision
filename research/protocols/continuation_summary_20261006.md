The estimand is the effect of the fixed random graph exposure continuation relative to equal-update base-only continuation, conditional on the three original faithful 100k parents and their paired frame/noise schedules. ## Hierarchy, paired effects and null propagation

Observed validation and test are separate populations; autonomous test is a third population. Particles/coordinates are averaged within each observed frame, frames equally within trajectory, and trajectories equally within original seed. Autonomous trajectory metrics are averaged equally over the 27 declared trajectories. Unequal validation histories per trajectory do not change equal trajectory weighting. Report all three explicit seed values, their mean and sample SD with ddof=1, without p-values or treating particles/frames/policy outcomes as independent replications.

For every fixed metric preserve absolute arm/policy values, five same-policy mix-minus-base arm effects, `(risk−random)_mix − (risk−random)_base`, and risk-minus-random, risk-minus-speed, dense-minus-base within each arm. Compute each two- or four-term difference at the identical frame/trajectory unit before hierarchical averaging. Cross-check the paired means against frozen `continuation_evaluation.paired_contrasts`; retain every unit difference and trajectory value beyond what that helper returns. Autonomous `laggedrisk25` and observed `previous-observed-base-risk25` are different controllers.

Any required undefined unit nulls its trajectory, seed and corresponding three-seed summary; show required/defined counts, every missing reason and contributing ID. Integrity-invalid families are ineligible, not ordinary scientific nulls. Committed failure indicators remain defined. Negative error/failure arm effects favor mix; negative allocation interaction improves risk relative to random after mixed graph exposure. Positive gain favors the action; correlation direction is associative. Display unfavorable, failed and null outcomes alongside favorable ones.
