# Physical descriptors versus saved residual risk: exploratory pilot

Written October 4, 2026, in response to the author's suggested Spearman diagnostic, before computing these associations. The pilot predictions, error/benefit outcomes, validation trajectories and first three test trajectories have already been inspected. This is an exploratory postprocess, not independent confirmation. It does not change the running full-model training, its final-checkpoint rule, or either locked full-model evaluation protocol.

Use all 15 existing compact models: faithful, corrected NLL and beta-NLL at seeds 0–4. Reuse every saved risk-benefit frame (36 validation and 36 test frames/model). Pin the existing summary SHA256 to the tracked bytes at commit d061cef in physical_complexity_inputs.json. Verify that root digest before loading the summary, then verify its raw-array, data and metadata hashes, frame identities, particle counts and common frame order across models. Load no checkpoints and no reserved full-test source. Geometry descriptors are computed once per observed frame and shared across every model.

At target index t, use x[t−1] and displacement v=x[t−1]−x[t−2]. Do not use target x[t] to construct descriptors. The last-three-observed-frame acceleration descriptor is the norm of x[t−1]−2x[t−2]+x[t−3]. Units are dataset coordinate units per stored frame (or per frame squared); these are not asserted SI units. The metadata sampling interval is retained as provenance; a constant positive time rescaling leaves rank correlations unchanged.

For each particle, form strict radius-0.015 neighbors in float64, without self pairs. Fit v[j]−v[i] ≈ L[i](x[j]−x[i]) by unweighted least squares, scaling positions by the fixed radius for conditioning. Require at least three neighbors, full spatial rank and condition number of the 2×2 Gram matrix at most 1e6. No ridge fallback, changing radius, fitted threshold or replacement of missing gradients is allowed.

Primary descriptors are strain Frobenius norm ||(L+Lᵀ)/2||F and absolute vorticity |L21−L12|. Absolute divergence |tr L| is a secondary descriptor, partly redundant with strain. These are kinematic descriptors, not ground-truth complexity or uncertainty. Also record neighbor count, speed, minimum signed box-plane clearance (negative outside the box), observed acceleration magnitude and RMS neighbor velocity difference. The box-plane clearance is not a detected free surface; neighbor count is sampling/crowding, not calibrated mass density.

Compute average-rank Spearman correlations within each frame. Primary, divergence and RMS neighbor-velocity-difference associations use the common geometry-identifiable mask, independent of objective/seed. On that same mask, retain risk versus saved base vector error and signed dense-intervention benefit for context. Simple speed, neighbor-count, wall-clearance and observed-acceleration correlations also use all particles, with explicit labels. The saved scalar risk is the positive base-graph variance, not a new score from a model call.

For each primary descriptor, add descriptive partial rank correlation: regress average ranks of both risk and descriptor on an intercept and average ranks of neighbor count, speed and box-plane clearance; correlate the two residual vectors. Use a fixed SVD least-squares tolerance of 1e−12, record effective rank and require at least three residual degrees of freedom. A residual norm no greater than 1e−10 times the original centered-rank norm is undefined. Constant vectors, fewer than three usable particles or insufficient residual degrees of freedom remain undefined. The transformed-neighbor-count variant is omitted because ranking log1p(count) is identical to ranking count. No particle-level p-values are reported and adjustment does not prove causality or remove every confounder.

Save per-frame coefficients, valid/invalid fit counts and fractions, and risk means for included/excluded particles. Average coefficients equally over the 12 frames per trajectory, then equally over the three trajectories per split. Report every seed, mean and sample SD over exactly five seeds. Unconditional summaries are null if any required frame coefficient is undefined. Separately report a clearly labeled defined-frame conditional mean, still giving equal weight to trajectories, with counts at each level. A conditional aggregate is still null if an entire required trajectory or seed has no defined frame; no trajectories or seeds are omitted from the requested three-trajectory/five-seed aggregate. Do not report survivor/defined-only numbers as unconditional results. Split-level correlations are averages of within-frame coefficients, not pooled particle correlations; seed SD does not represent uncertainty over the physical trajectory population.

Preserve all objectives, splits, proxies, signs and undefined cases. No favorable metric/radius selection, model training, checkpoint selection, allocation-policy modification or performance/speedup conclusion follows from this diagnostic. Positive spatial alignment alone cannot show that expanding high-risk regions improves predictions. Existing risk–error and risk–benefit measurements and the locked full-model policy comparisons remain necessary.

Save source/protocol/data/raw-input hashes, descriptor arrays, frame identities, software versions and CPU postprocessing time. Postprocessing time is not policy inference cost. Keep raw cached geometry descriptors under work/ and curate scalar results/figures into the research fork. Any later application to completed full-model artifacts must be separately documented and must wait for the existing completion/test gate; it is not authorized to alter the locked five-policy results or their timing.
